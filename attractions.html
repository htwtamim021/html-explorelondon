Famous Attractions in London

London has many interesting places to see.

Buckingham Palace

Buckingham Palace is a famous royal building. Many tourists visit the area and watch the Changing of the Guard.

The London Eye

The London Eye is a large observation wheel beside the River Thames. It offers views over the city.

Tower of London

The Tower of London is a historic castle. Visitors can learn about its history and see the Crown Jewels.
